Simple Demos

Demos built without any libraries or frameworks — just pure CSS, HTML, and JS.

  • A JS-less Neural Network on… Scrollbars — a neural network trained live by pure CSS: scroll positions as parameters, mandatory snap as the optimizer, the render loop as the clock. A goldfish learns to swim. Zero JavaScript.
  • Blackbody Candle — a candle you can light, where nothing about the flame is hand-drawn: a reacting, buoyant gas simulation gives it its shape and Planck’s law seen through the CIE 1931 observer gives it its colour. Rust compiled to WebAssembly, drawn by JavaScript.
  • A Random-Post Button, Now With One (1) Link — a CSS-only random-link button: three ways to fake randomness without random(), plus a rolling 3D die.
