Simple Demos

Demos built without any libraries or frameworks — just pure CSS, HTML, and JS.

  • Precisely random(): Integrals in Pure CSS — Monte Carlo integration in pure CSS: a thousand darts placed by random() and tallied by CSS counters, plus one element that keeps throwing forever and counts in animation time. π, and integrals with no antiderivative at all. Zero JavaScript.
  • A JS-less Neural Network on… Scrollbars — a neural network trained live by pure CSS: scroll positions as parameters, mandatory snap as the optimizer, the render loop as the clock. A goldfish learns to swim. Zero JavaScript.
  • How Many Rust Engines Does It Take to Light a Candle? — one, it turns out, plus a little bit of physics: a simulation of the hot gas above the wick, and some rendering that turns it into light you can actually see, all packed into a WebAssembly engine written in Rust, plus a couple of WebGL shaders that put the pixels on screen.
  • A Random-Post Button, Now With One (1) Link — a CSS-only random-link button: three ways to fake randomness without random(), plus a rolling 3D die.
