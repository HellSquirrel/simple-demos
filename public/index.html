Simple Demos

Demos built without any libraries or frameworks — just pure CSS, HTML, and JS.

  • Precisely random(): Integrals in Pure CSS — Can we do calculus in pure CSS? Easy, especially now that CSS has random(). Throw confetti, count what lands under the curve, and out come π and integrals with no elementary antiderivative.
  • A JS-less Neural Network on… Scrollbars — a neural network trained live by pure CSS: scroll positions as parameters, mandatory snap as the optimizer, the render loop as the clock. A goldfish learns to swim. Zero JavaScript.
  • How Many Rust Engines Does It Take to Light a Candle? — one, it turns out, plus a little bit of physics: a simulation of the hot gas above the wick, and some rendering that turns it into light you can actually see, all packed into a WebAssembly engine written in Rust, plus a couple of WebGL shaders that put the pixels on screen. Inspired by Carmen Ansio’s A flame needs three sine waves.
  • A Random-Post Button, Now With One (1) Link — a CSS-only random-link button: three ways to fake randomness without random(), plus a rolling 3D die. Inspired by Chris Coyier’s A Button That Links to a Random Post in HTML & CSS Only.
